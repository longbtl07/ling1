## 📚 Key Modules

### Module 1: Core Knowledge Review (Lý Thuyết Trọng Tâm)
1. **Morphology (Hình thái học):**
   * **Morpheme Hierarchy Tree:** `MORPHEME` ➔ `FREE MORPHEME` (`CONTENT WORD` vs `FUNCTION WORD`) & `BOUND MORPHEME` (`AFFIX` vs `BOUND ROOT`).
   * **Mandatory Rules & Usage Conditions:**
     * Derivational affixes attach closer to the root than inflectional affixes (`teach-er-s`, not `*teach-s-er`).
     * Inflectional affixes never change part of speech.
     * Strictly 8 inflectional suffixes in English (`-s` pl, `-'s` poss, `-s` 3rd pres, `-ed` past, `-en/ed` past part, `-ing` pres part, `-er` compr, `-est` supl).
   * **Niche & Special Cases:** Bound roots (`-ceive`, `-struct`), Enclitics (`'ll`, `'ve`, `-n't`), Zero Morphemes & Suppletion (`{SHEEP} + {pl}`, `{WRITE} + {past}`, `{LITTLE} + {supl}`).
   * **ULIS Analysis Rubric:**
     * *Morphemic Analysis* (Abstract morphemes in ALL CAPS in curly braces with mandatory `{sg}` and `{pres}`).
     * *Morphological Analysis* (Overt surface cuts with slashes and classifications).
   * **8 Word Formation Processes & Subtypes:** Derivation, Reduplication (Exact, Ablaut, Rhyme), Conversion/Functional Shift (Commonization, Stress Shift), Compounding (Leftmost stress, Headness Rule), Blending, Back-formation, Shortening (Clipping, Acronyms, Initialism), Root Creation (Onomatopoeia, Literary Coinage, Brand Names).

2. **Phonetics & Phonology (Ngữ âm & Âm vị học):**
   * **Vocal Organs & Sound Production:** Larynx, Vocal Cords (Voiced vs Voiceless), Pharynx, Soft Palate/Velum (Oral vs Nasal sounds), Glottis.
   * **Consonant 3-Criteria Formula:** `[Voicing] + [Place of Articulation] + [Manner of Articulation]` (8 places, 6 manners).
   * **Monophthong 4-Criteria Formula:** `[Length] + [Tongue Height] + [Tongue Backness] + [Lip Rounding]` (12 monophthongs + 8 diphthongs).
   * **Phonology:** Phonemes `/.../` vs Allophones `[...]`, Complementary vs Contrastive distribution, Zero-trap Minimal Pair Matrix.
   * **Syllables & Suprasegmentals:** Internal syllable structure ($\sigma \to \text{Onset} + \text{Rhyme} \to \text{Nucleus} + \text{Coda}$), Maximum Onset Principle (MOP) algorithm, and Grammatical Stress Shift.

### Module 2: Interactive Quiz Bank (Kho Đề Thi Thử 42 Câu)
42 brand-new practice questions split across 8 official exam sections:
* **Section 1 (5 Qs):** Term Identification (no more than 2 words)
* **Section 2 (6 Qs):** True / False Conceptual Traps
* **Section 3 (5 Qs):** Phonemic Transcription & Sound Description
* **Section 4 (5 Qs):** Sound Symbols from Description
* **Section 5 (5 Qs):** Minimal Pairs Construction & Validation
* **Section 6 (5 Qs):** Morphemic Analysis (ULIS braces format)
* **Section 7 (5 Qs):** Morphological Analysis (Slash cuts & labels)
* **Section 8 (6 Qs):** Word Formation Process Identification

**Interactive Features:**
* **Instant Evaluation:** Instant green/red visual response with model answers, theoretical rationale, and exam trap alerts.
* **Score Summary Dashboard:** Live tracking of completion percentage, correct/wrong counts, and quick jump to unanswered questions.

## 🌐 Enabling GitHub Pages

To host this web application for free on GitHub Pages:
1. Go to your repository on GitHub: `https://github.com/longbtl07/ling1`
2. Navigate to **Settings** ➔ **Pages** (under "Code and automation").
3. Under **Build and deployment** ➔ **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Choose Branch: `main` and Folder: `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
5. Within 1–2 minutes, your website will be live at:
   **`https://longbtl07.github.io/ling1/`**
